Privacy Policy

This page tells you what hammerstreetclean.org collects, who handles it, and what you can ask us to do with it. You do not need an account to use this site, and you can report an issue anonymously.

Issue Reports

The report form sends us the issue type, location and description that you enter. A severity level and a photo are optional.

Your name, phone and email are optional. We use them only to follow up on your report.

If you report a tagged business: we can tell that business it was tagged, and we say that "a member of our community" notified us. We never give them your name.

Location: your browser asks for permission to read your location. If you allow it, the form shows your coordinates on your screen. The form does not send those coordinates to us. You can refuse and type an address.

Photos: we store the file as you upload it, at a long random web address that we do not publish. Anyone who has that address can open the photo. A photo file can contain the place and time it was taken and the device used. Remove that data before you upload if you do not want us to have it, and avoid photos that show people's faces or licence plates.

Messages, Payments and Visits

Contact form: we receive your name, email, reason and message, plus a phone number and location if you give them. We use these to reply and to do the work you ask about.

Payments: this website does not collect payment details. Memberships and coffees go through Buy Me a Coffee, under its own privacy policy. E-Transfers go through your bank.

Visits: our own code does not set cookies. We use Google Analytics to count visitors and to see which sites and general areas they come from. The issue report page does not load Google Analytics. The services listed below can receive your IP address and browser details when a page loads.

We do not sell your personal information. We share your contact details only with the service providers below, or if the law requires it.

Advertising, Analytics and Cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this site or other sites. Google and its partners use advertising cookies to show you ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

This site also uses Google Analytics, which uses cookies to measure visits. It sends Google information such as the pages you view, the site that sent you and your browser details. We see the results as visitor reports.

  • Stop Google Analytics from measuring your visits with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Turn off personalized ads from Google at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of personalized ad cookies from some other vendors at aboutads.info/choices or, in Canada, youradchoices.ca.
  • Read how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

How Long We Keep It

We keep reports, photos and messages while we need them for the work and for our business records. You can ask us to delete yours at any time. We will tell you if we must keep something, for example an invoice record.

Your Choices

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Send your request to contact@hammerstreetclean.org. If you reported anonymously, give us the reference number so that we can find the report.

If we do not resolve a privacy concern, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
